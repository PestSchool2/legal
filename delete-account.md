# Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your PestPic account from inside the app:
**Settings → Delete account**. You are signed out as soon as you confirm, and it needs nothing
from us.

If you no longer have the app installed, email **Thepestpic@gmail.com** from the Google account you
signed up with, with the subject **Delete my account**.

Sending it from that account is what proves the request is yours, so we do not have to ask you for
anything else. We will confirm by reply once the request is in place.

## What happens when you ask

You are signed out right away and the account can no longer be used. The data listed below is erased
**30 days after your request**, not at the moment you make it.

## Changed your mind

Sign in again with the same Google or Apple account within those 30 days and the deletion request is
cancelled: your account and everything in it come back exactly as they were. Signing in is all it
takes — there is no separate recovery form and nothing to email us about.

Once the 30 days have passed, the deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and the email address attached to it
- Every diagnosis in your history, and the photos you uploaded with them
- The phone number and address you entered when contacting a pest control company

All of it is erased 30 days after your request, unless you sign in again before then.

## What does not get deleted

Pest control companies you have already contacted received your request by email at the time you
sent it. That mail is theirs, not ours, and deleting your account does not reach into their inbox.
Contact the company directly if you want it removed.

We also keep records we are legally required to keep, for as long as the law requires.
